EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This Employment Contract is made and entered into on December 27, 2025, by and between:

EMPLOYER: Tech Solutions Private Limited
Address: 123 Business Park, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
(hereinafter referred to as "Employer")

AND

EMPLOYEE: Rahul Sharma
Address: 456 Residential Complex, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
(hereinafter referred to as "Employee")

RECITALS:
The Employer desires to employ the Employee, and the Employee desires to accept such employment under the terms and conditions set forth in this Contract.

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
1.1 The Employee is hired as Senior Software Engineer.
1.2 The Employee shall perform all duties assigned by the Employer including software development, code review, and team collaboration.
1.3 The Employee shall devote their full professional time and attention to the performance of their duties.

2. COMPENSATION
2.1 Base Salary: The Employer shall pay the Employee an annual salary of ₹1,200,000 (Rupees Twelve Lakhs only), payable in monthly installments of ₹100,000.
2.2 Bonus: The Employee may be eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 20% of base salary.
2.3 Benefits: The Employee shall be entitled to health insurance, provident fund contributions, and other benefits as per company policy.

3. TERM OF EMPLOYMENT
3.1 This Contract shall commence on January 1, 2026.
3.2 The employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party with 60 days written notice.
3.3 During probation (first 6 months), the notice period shall be 30 days.

4. WORKING HOURS
4.1 Normal working hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
4.2 The Employee may be required to work additional hours as needed.
4.3 Flexible work arrangements may be provided at the Employer's discretion.

5. LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
5.1 Annual Leave: 24 days per year
5.2 Sick Leave: 12 days per year
5.3 Casual Leave: 8 days per year
5.4 Earned leave can be carried forward to the next year subject to company policy.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE
6.1 The Employee agrees to maintain confidentiality of all proprietary information.
6.2 This obligation continues even after termination of employment.
6.3 The Employee shall not disclose any trade secrets or confidential business information.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
7.1 All work product created during employment shall be the exclusive property of the Employer.
7.2 The Employee assigns all rights, title, and interest in any inventions or creations to the Employer.

8. NON-COMPETE CLAUSE
8.1 During employment and for 12 months thereafter, the Employee shall not:
   - Work for direct competitors
   - Solicit Employer's clients
   - Start competing business
8.2 This clause is limited to the geographical area of Mumbai and Pune.

9. TERMINATION
9.1 Termination for Cause: The Employer may terminate immediately for misconduct, breach of contract, or poor performance.
9.2 Termination without Cause: Either party may terminate with 60 days notice.
9.3 Upon termination, the Employee must return all company property.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
10.1 Any disputes shall first be attempted to be resolved through mediation.
10.2 If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved through arbitration in Mumbai.
10.3 The arbitration shall be conducted under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This Contract shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to the jurisdiction of Mumbai courts.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Contract constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, whether oral or written.
