FORENSIC AUDIT REPORT

CASE NO: 2025/CR/123
CLIENT: Kapoor Enterprises Private Limited
PERIOD UNDER REVIEW: January 2024 - September 2025
AUDIT FIRM: Sharma & Associates, Chartered Accountants
DATE: November 20, 2025

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This forensic audit was conducted to investigate suspected financial irregularities at Kapoor Enterprises Private Limited. The investigation revealed systematic embezzlement of company funds totaling ₹25,00,000 over a period of 15 months.

1. SCOPE OF AUDIT:
The audit covered:
- All bank accounts of Kapoor Enterprises
- Cash transactions and petty cash records
- Accounting ledgers and journals
- Electronic payment systems
- Safe deposit transactions
- Vendor and supplier records

2. METHODOLOGY:
Our investigation included:
- Document verification and analysis
- Bank statement reconciliation
- Interview with company staff
- Digital forensics of computer systems
- Cross-verification of invoices and payments
- CCTV footage review

3. KEY FINDINGS:

3.1 UNAUTHORIZED WITHDRAWALS:
Between June 2024 and September 2025, a total of 47 unauthorized cash withdrawals were made from the company's primary bank account:
- June 2024: 3 withdrawals totaling ₹1,50,000
- July 2024: 4 withdrawals totaling ₹2,00,000
- August 2024: 5 withdrawals totaling ₹2,50,000
- September 2024: 4 withdrawals totaling ₹2,00,000
- October 2024: 5 withdrawals totaling ₹2,50,000
- November 2024: 4 withdrawals totaling ₹2,00,000
- December 2024: 5 withdrawals totaling ₹2,50,000
- January 2025: 4 withdrawals totaling ₹2,00,000
- February 2025: 4 withdrawals totaling ₹2,00,000
- March 2025: 3 withdrawals totaling ₹1,50,000
- April 2025: 3 withdrawals totaling ₹1,50,000
- May 2025: 2 withdrawals totaling ₹1,00,000
- June 2025: 1 withdrawal totaling ₹50,000
- July 2025: 2 withdrawals totaling ₹1,00,000
- August 2025: 2 withdrawals totaling ₹1,00,000
- September 2025: 1 withdrawal totaling ₹50,000

TOTAL: ₹25,00,000

3.2 FALSIFICATION OF RECORDS:
Mr. Rajesh Malhotra, who had access to the accounting system, made the following manipulations:
- Created 47 fake vendor entries
- Generated false invoices to justify cash withdrawals
- Altered ledger entries to balance the books
- Deleted email evidence (partially recovered through forensic methods)

3.3 PATTERN OF FRAUD:
The fraud followed a consistent pattern:
1. Cash withdrawal made using authorized signatory powers
2. Fake vendor invoice created within 24-48 hours
3. Ledger entry made to show legitimate business expense
4. Supporting documents forged or fabricated
5. Evidence of withdrawal concealed in routine business transactions

3.4 DIGITAL EVIDENCE:
Computer forensics revealed:
- 156 deleted emails related to financial transactions
- Browser history showing access to fake invoice generators
- WhatsApp messages discussing the fraud (recovered from backup)
- Metadata showing document creation dates didn't match transaction dates

4. AUTHENTICATION OF DOCUMENTS:

4.1 GENUINE TRANSACTIONS:
We verified 1,247 legitimate business transactions during the period, which were properly documented and authorized.

4.2 FRAUDULENT TRANSACTIONS:
All 47 suspicious transactions were confirmed as fraudulent based on:
- No actual vendor services received
- Fake vendor registration details
- Forged authorization signatures
- Inconsistent documentation

5. FINANCIAL IMPACT:

Direct Loss: ₹25,00,000 (embezzled amount)
Indirect Losses:
- Interest loss on missing funds: ₹1,50,000
- Lost business opportunities: ₹10,00,000
- Audit costs: ₹2,50,000
- Legal costs: ₹3,00,000
- Reputation damage: Unquantifiable

TOTAL QUANTIFIABLE LOSS: ₹42,00,000

6. TIMELINE OF EVENTS:

March 2024: Mr. Malhotra appointed as financial controller
May 2024: First unauthorized access to safe observed (CCTV)
June 2024: First fraudulent withdrawal of ₹50,000
June-Sept 2024: Systematic increase in withdrawal amounts
Oct 2024-Aug 2025: Regular monthly withdrawals
Sept 2025: Last fraudulent transaction
Oct 1, 2025: Fraud discovered during routine audit
Oct 5, 2025: Mr. Malhotra confronted and admitted guilt
Oct 8, 2025: Police complaint filed
Oct 10, 2025: Accused arrested

7. EVIDENCE PRESERVED:

All evidence has been secured and documented:
- Bank statements (certified copies)
- Original invoices and ledgers
- Computer hard drive images
- Email server backups
- CCTV footage (DVR copies)
- WhatsApp chat records
- Witness statements from staff

8. RECOMMENDATIONS:

To prevent future fraud:
- Implement dual authorization for transactions above ₹50,000
- Regular internal audits (quarterly)
- Rotation of financial staff duties
- Enhanced background checks for financial positions
- Implementation of accounting software with audit trails
- Regular reconciliation of accounts
- CCTV monitoring with alerts
- Surprise cash counts

9. CONCLUSION:

Based on our forensic examination, we conclusively determine that:
1. Mr. Rajesh Malhotra systematically embezzled ₹25,00,000 from Kapoor Enterprises
2. The fraud was premeditated and executed over 15 months
3. Multiple methods were used to conceal the fraud
4. The evidence is substantial and admissible in court
5. Internal control weaknesses facilitated the fraud
