STATEMENT UNDER OATH:

I, Mrs. Priya Kapoor, do hereby solemnly affirm and state as follows:

1. BACKGROUND:
I am the owner and managing director of Kapoor Enterprises Private Limited, a company engaged in import-export business. I have known the accused, Mr. Rajesh Malhotra, for approximately 5 years through business dealings.

2. BUSINESS RELATIONSHIP:
In March 2024, I entered into a business partnership with Mr. Malhotra wherein he was entrusted with handling our company's financial transactions and maintaining accounts.

3. DISCOVERY OF FRAUD:
On October 1, 2025, during a routine audit, our chartered accountant discovered discrepancies in the accounts. Upon investigation, it was found that:
- Approximately ₹25,00,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Lakhs) was missing from company accounts
- Multiple unauthorized withdrawals were made between June 2024 and September 2025
- False entries were made in the ledger to conceal the transactions

4. EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
I have in my possession the following documents:
a) Bank statements showing unauthorized withdrawals
b) Company ledger with false entries
c) Email communications with the accused
d) CCTV footage from our office showing the accused accessing the safe
e) Chartered Accountant's audit report

5. CONFRONTATION:
On October 5, 2025, I confronted Mr. Malhotra about these discrepancies. He initially denied any wrongdoing but later admitted to taking the money, claiming he had "borrowed" it temporarily and would return it. However, no repayment has been made to date.

6. LOSSES INCURRED:
Due to this breach of trust:
- Our company suffered financial loss of ₹25,00,000
- Business operations were disrupted
- We lost two major contracts due to cash flow problems
- Company reputation was damaged
- Mental stress and anxiety to me and my family

7. POLICE COMPLAINT:
I filed a police complaint (FIR No. 456/2025) at Marine Drive Police Station on October 8, 2025, under the following sections:
- Section 406 IPC (Criminal Breach of Trust)
- Section 420 IPC (Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property)

8. CURRENT STATUS:
The accused was arrested on October 10, 2025, and is currently out on bail. I am seeking:
- Return of the embezzled amount of ₹25,00,000
- Interest on the amount from June 2024
- Compensation for business losses (approximately ₹10,00,000)
- Punishment as per law

9. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
During the investigation, it was discovered that the accused had similar cases filed against him in Delhi and Bangalore for financial fraud, though he never disclosed this to us.

10. PRAYER:
I humbly request this Honorable Court to:
- Take cognizance of this serious breach of trust
- Ensure the accused is held accountable
- Order compensation for the losses suffered
- Take appropriate action to prevent the accused from committing similar crimes

I declare that the above statement is true to the best of my knowledge and belief, and nothing has been concealed or misstated.
